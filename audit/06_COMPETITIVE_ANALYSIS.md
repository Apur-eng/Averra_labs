## 2. Distinctive Identity Opportunities for Averra Labs

Rather than imitating Western creative-tech studios with abstract WebGL or unshipped R&D slogans, Averra has a **clear, authentic moat** evidenced by its actual portfolio:

1. **"Full-Stack Business Systems for Growth-Stage Indian Enterprises & Institutions":**
   - Averra has already shipped **multi-portal fintech ERPs** (`Finservkart` — 68 pages, 7 roles), **cross-platform mobile/web LMS apps** (`Fundamics LMS` — React Native + Expo), **transactional e-commerce** (`Mokshita` — Razorpay + PostgreSQL), and **high-speed local/healthcare flagships** (`Solasta`, `Feel Fresh`).
   - Few boutique Indian studios pair **clean editorial UI** with **hardened multi-role operational software** (RBAC, payments, mobile apps).
2. **Productized Transparency Without Cheapening the Brand:**
   - Replace `₹8,000` entry-level brochure pricing on `/contact` (or separate a dedicated *"Starter Flagship"* tier from *"Custom Systems & Apps"* starting at realistic engineering retainers) so enterprise/LMS/SaaS buyers take Averra seriously.
3. **Show Real Shipped Artifacts Instead of Placeholder Slots:**
   - Replace `"INTRO VIDEO — Future Insertion Slot"` with a crisp 15-second screen-recorded product reel of `Finservkart`, `Fundamics LMS`, and `Mokshita`, and publish 1–2 real AI/SaaS case studies (or remove empty filter tabs until shipped).
4. **Founder-Led Technical Authority:**
   - On `/know-us`, `TEAM_MEMBERS` lists only names (`Apurv Singh`, `Abhinav Tiwari`, `Ritesh Singh`) without roles, specialties, or links. Adding engineering roles, GitHub/LinkedIn links, and real client attributions turns "small team" into a high-trust differentiator.
