## 1. Executive Summary
Solasta Life Sciences Pvt. Ltd. is a super-specialty pharmaceutical company headquartered in New Delhi with WHO-GMP certified manufacturing facilities. Averra developed a corporate digital flagship and formulation catalog providing healthcare practitioners and medical distributors instant access to critical medicine specifications across five core therapeutic areas.

## 2. Verified Technical Architecture

### 2.1 Corporate Presentation & Structure
- **Live Deployment Domain**: `https://solasta-clone-website.vercel.app/` [VERIFIED BY USER]
- **Visual Assets**: Authentically integrated in Averra portfolio (`/images/projects/solasta/torasta_10.png`, `sevasta_400.png`, `ketocut.png`, `sodasta_500.png`, `renibus_heal.png`).
- **Data Layer**: Flat-file JSON architecture (`solasta_data.json`) storing complete drug formulation specifications.
- **Formulation Portfolio**:
  - *Nephrology*: Torasta (Torsemide 10/20/40/100mg), Sevasta (Sevelamer Carbonate 400/800mg), Sodasta (Sodium Bicarbonate), Ketocut (Alpha Ketoanalogue).
  - Complete with generic composition, pack sizes (e.g. `10 x 10 Tablets`), and clinical indications.

### 2.2 Frontend & Design System
- **Languages**: Semantic HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, Vanilla CSS.
- **Typography Scale**: Google Fonts `Inter` (body paragraphs) and `Outfit` (bold display headlines).
- **Motion & Interaction**:
  - AOS (Animate On Scroll) `2.3.4` triggers for section entrance.
  - Interactive category tab filters that swap product grids instantly on client side.
  - Accessible modal inspector for detailed formulation viewing without navigating away.
- **Corporate Portals**:
  - `careers.html`: Talent acquisition application with department routing.
  - `contact.html`: Corporate office inquiries and direct communications.
