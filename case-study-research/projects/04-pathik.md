## 2. Verified Technical Architecture

### 2.1 600vh Canvas Scrollytelling
- **Framework**: HTML5 `<canvas>` + GSAP (GreenSock) + ScrollTrigger.
- **Media Ingestion**: Preloaded sequence of 90+ optimized `.webp` frame images (`frame_00_delay-0.125s.jpg` etc.) depicting morning mist and crop walks, eliminating video streaming buffers on low-bandwidth rural mobile connections.
- **Dynamic Header**: Glassmorphism header that remains transparent during the canvas sequence and transitions to frosted glass upon reaching standard page content.

### 2.2 Interactive Features
- **Bilingual Interface**: English / Hindi toggle dynamically switching text strings across the interface.
- **Before/After Soil Slider**: Drag comparison slider contrasting chemical-degraded soil with revitalized organic farm yields.
- **Learning Hub**: Filterable library of natural compost recipes and video tutorials.
- **Cohort Funnel**: Dedicated enrollment page for the March 2026 organic masterclass cohort.
